Getting started

ARIA is a Star Citizen assistant that runs on your own PC. Nothing goes to a server of ours: it pulls public prices from UEX Corp and game data from the Star Citizen Wiki, caches them next to the app, and does the rest locally.

  • Install: unzip, double-click install.bat. It installs Python if the PC has none, puts ARIA in your Start menu and on the desktop. From then on, click the ARIA shortcut — it updates itself from GitHub on every launch.
  • First look: the boot screen loads prices and ships, then shows Continue (or Update when there's a newer version). The five tabs across the top are the rooms; the ⚙ is Settings; ⟳ pulls fresh data.
  • Set your pilot name and home base once — Settings ⚙ → Assistant. Home is where RTB takes you (Port Tressler, New Babbage, wherever you spawn).
  • Starting order doesn't matter. Start ARIA before or after the RSI launcher, in the menus or in game — she finds the Star Citizen window when she needs to press a key. The deck strip shows SC LINKED when the game is running. The one thing to do with the game closed is keybind changes (bind … → vJoy): while it runs they queue up and apply when you quit.
  • PRACTICE vs KEYS LIVE on the deck strip isn't about the game — it's the Press keys in game switch in the Voice room. PRACTICE = ARIA says what she'd press but presses nothing. Tick it once you trust a macro.
  • Pills in the header: VOICE (click to toggle listening), UEX / WIKI (green = live, amber = cached, red = source down — ARIA keeps working from the last copy), COCKPIT (click or F9).
You don't need the MFDs, voice, VR or any of the cockpit stuff to use ARIA. Typing in the ask bar is the whole game: "cutlass black 150k" is a complete request.

Make Me Rich

Type a ship and a budget, press ENGAGE (or Enter). ARIA ranks the hauls by total profit from live terminal prices and stock, and tells you in plain words what to buy, where, and where to sell.

Each result row has: ⌖ show this run on the starmap · + Job add it to your job list · Next leg reinvest from the sell point (ARIA finds the best haul starting where you just sold, with the money you now have).

The filters under the bar: Legal cargo only, Avoid volatile, Stay in one system, and Hide prices older than N days (UEX prices are player-reported; the DATA cell shows how old they are — glance at the terminal before you commit a big outlay).

The ship box is an A–Z picker: start typing, pick from the list, or add a custom ship with its own SCU. The Cargo SCU box overrides the ship's listed capacity any time.

Jobs, Home & RTB

+ Job on any haul adds it to the job bar (bottom). Jobs chain: buy here, sell there, buy again, sell again, then RTB caps the list with the trip home. The job bar has from Home (start the plan from your base) and RTB toggles, and View job on map opens the Job view: systems as cards linked by jump points, the route running through them with prices on every stop.

Home is set in Settings → Assistant or by clicking the ⌂ next to "Starting from". Change it whenever you move spawn.

Shipyard & Blueprints

Shipyard: every flyable ship from the game files, with cargo, crew, size, speeds, and in-game buy / rent prices from UEX. ★ a ship to put it in My Hangar; hangar ships show up as one-click picks in Make Me Rich. Tick up to four and Compare them side by side.

Blueprint Room: every crafting blueprint, its materials and which missions drop it. Mark the ones you own; queue what you want to build and ARIA totals the materials and finds the cheapest terminal for each.

Hangar, owned blueprints and the build queue live in your browser. Settings → Your stuff exports a backup you can import on another PC.

Starmap & Nav

Every system, planet, moon, station and jump point from the game files. Click a body for its terminals and best trades. Market overlay picks a commodity and lights where it's bought (green) and sold (red).

Plot a course: "nav from area18 to pyro gateway" or the Nav panel. ARIA finds the fastest path across quantum hops and jump points for your ship's QT drive and gives legs, ETA and jumps. The ⌖ on any haul draws that run.

Job view (from the job bar) is the same map laid out as system cards along your route.

Mining & refining

The Mining tab. The ore board lists every minable commodity UEX knows (ship ore, hand-mined gems, gases/salvage) with the best place to sell it raw and the best place to sell it refined, per SCU, and the uplift between the two. Filter by system or kind, search by name, click an ore for every terminal that buys it with the price age.

What are my bags worth? — type what your cargo readout shows (SCU per mineral). ARIA totals selling raw vs refining first (at the yield % you set — fast, cheap methods lose material; slow, dear ones keep it) and names the best terminal for each, then gives a verdict. The refinery fee isn't in UEX's data, so it's "before the fee".

Refineries — every refinery deck with location, UEX's per-ore yield bonus (the best one for the selected ore is outlined), and the processing methods with their yield / cost / speed ratings.

Field notes are the parts no API has — scan, green window, inert, Quantainium's timer — plus a notes box of your own that stays on your PC. Verify the notes against the current patch; they drift.

Voice & push-to-talk

Voice Macros room. ARIA listens in Chrome/Edge (the app window already is one). Two listening modes:

  • Push-to-talk (recommended): press Set button, then press the key, mouse button or HOTAS/joystick button you want. ARIA only hears you while it's held — Discord chatter is ignored.
  • Always on: tick Wake word so only "aria, …" counts.

Macros: a phrase → keys ARIA presses in game → what she replies. "call atc" → lctrl+n → "Hailing station." Key syntax: lctrl+n, b:1.5 (hold 1.5 s), wait 0.4, mouse1, f13…f24, vj12 (vJoy button 12). Press Save profile; macros live in macros.json.

Press keys in game must be ticked for ARIA to actually press anything; off = practice mode (she says what she'd press). ARIA hands focus back to Star Citizen before pressing, so run the game borderless windowed, not exclusive fullscreen.

Replies: browser voice, your own voice-pack clips (drop audio files in web/voice/, named after the line — Export voice lines lists the file names), or silent. ARIA volume slider sets how loud she is. The Mic chip shows permission; Allow microphone asks for it; the picker + Test level shows a meter for each mic. Chrome's recognizer always uses the Windows default mic — Windows sound settings opens the page to change that.

Anything you say that isn't a macro goes to ARIA as a normal request: "aria, c2 hercules 2 million make me rich".

Keybinds & vJoy

"bind center turret" (typed or spoken) finds the game action by name — exact match, closest match, or "none" — shows what it's bound to now, and offers to bind it to a vJoy virtual button so none of your real keys change. ARIA edits your actionmaps.xml with a backup every time; while the game is running the change is queued and applied when you quit. One-time setup: install the free vJoy driver, and in Star Citizen's keybind options note which joystick number vJoy shows as (Settings → vJoy joystick number if ARIA can't detect it).

A macro can then press vj12 and the game sees "joystick 3 button 12". Hail target on a voice command, no keyboard involved.

Cockpit mode

Press ▣ in the header or F9. The page rearranges itself around the Thrustmaster Cougar MFD bezels on your monitor: left MFD, right MFD, the center MFD between them, and the bottom band with the header, ARIA's deck strip, the ask bar and the tabs. F9 again (or HOME) returns to the normal layout. Settings → Open ARIA on picks the monitor and full screen.

The Cougars are found by their USB id. The 20 buttons around each frame (OSBs) press the labels on screen with no mouse and no focus change, so the game keeps focus. If SYS says NOT FOUND: SYS → SET L / SET R and press any button on that MFD. SYS → TEST shows button numbers.

The bottom-right dock always has ⌂ HOME (leave cockpit mode), VR and ⌗ ALIGN.

Lining up the screens

ALGN on the center MFD, the ⌗ dock button, or Settings → Calibrate. Each area is a free box with eight crop handles like Photoshop: edge handles move one side, corners move two, drag the middle to move the whole thing. Ctrl = one side only, Shift = keep shape, Ctrl+Z undo, Reset this / Reset all, Esc or Done to finish. The floating panel has sliders and 1-px nudges, and for the three MFDs: button spacing, label inset and label size, with the MFD showing its button numbers while selected. Drag the panel anywhere by its header.

Do the left and right MFDs first (match the labels to the physical buttons), then the center, then the bottom band. What's inside each box scales to fit it.

Center MFD & deck

The middle screen is a third MFD with the same pages, driven by mouse (or VR) since it has no bezel. Its UFC page shows the route ARIA last answered — cash / time / stops / jumps / dist / fuel — and a mini starmap, with ACPT (make it a job), NEXT (reinvest leg), RTB, MAP, SAY, LOG (last 60 key presses ARIA sent) and DIM on the OSBs. SET, VR, ALGN, HELP and HOME live on every MFD's MENU page.

What ARIA says prints teletype-style on the deck strip in the bottom band, above the ask bar, with her status lamp (KEYS LIVE / PRACTICE, PTT / OPEN MIC) and SAY / RTB / MAP keys.

Skins & dimmer

Settings → Cockpit skin: ARIA stock, Drake, RSI, Anvil, Origin, Aegis, MISC, Crusader, or your own colours. Brightness (also DIM on the center MFD and the MFD rockers), bloom, scanlines. Each skin has a personality: Analog (radar sweep, needle bars), Smooth (fades, no flicker), Glitch (CRT fuzz, tears, jitter), with a strength slider.

Arcade & squad

MENU → GAME on any MFD. Pick a game on the side buttons; rockers are the d-pad (left pair up/down, right pair left/right), lower rockers are A and B; keyboard arrows / space / shift work when that MFD is the SOI. NEW, PAUSE, TOP10 on the top row. Every game has a top-10 board under your pilot name.

  • CHESS vs ARIA (four levels, UNDO) — rockers move the cursor, legal moves land on the OSBs.
  • CARGO (tetris): A rotates, B hard-drops. RUNNER (dino): A jumps, down ducks. ROIDS: turn, thrust, A fires, B hyperspace.
  • TANKS: artillery. Up/down angle, left/right power (hold to sweep), A fires, B arms your one nuke. Wind changes every turn.
  • FLEET: battleship. SHUF reshuffles your ships, READY locks in, d-pad aims, A fires.

Squad leaderboards

One of you hosts: Settings ⚙ → Squad → Host the squad board on this PC, restart ARIA once, give friends the address and key shown. Friends: Settings → Squad → paste both → Test. From then on every score posts to the host and every TOP10 is the whole squad (your entries ◀). Same LAN or a VPN (Tailscale) needs nothing; over the open internet the host forwards port 8777. A box that only hosts can run squad_server.py instead of the whole app (install_squad.bat).

Versus — GAME → SQUAD

Host a lobby (CHESS / TANKS / FLEET) or JOIN one listed. The game opens on both players' MFDs when the second seat fills; every move goes through the host so both screens replay the same game. Flip to another page mid-match and it waits — SQUAD → PLAY brings it back. RESGN resigns. The winner's score lands on that game's board as "vs NAME". No credits, no wagers.

VR

VR on the center MFD, the dock, or Settings → Enter VR puts the three MFDs and the bottom band in a floating arc in your headset. Point a controller, pull the trigger to click; squeeze the grip to recenter. Cougar buttons, voice and PTT keep working. Settings → VR has distance, height, spread, size and tilt, live.

It takes over the headset — it is not an overlay. Only enter when no game is running in VR (Star Citizen on your monitor + ARIA in the headset is the intended use). If a game is in the headset, it loses it (grey void) and can crash. To see ARIA inside a VR game, pin the ARIA window with an overlay tool — Desktop+ (free, SteamVR), XSOverlay or OVR Toolkit.

Needs an OpenXR runtime running (SteamVR, Pimax Play, Meta). Windows Mixed Reality headsets only work on Windows builds before 24H2.

Installing & sharing

  • Requirements: Windows 10/11, Chrome or Edge, Python 3.9+ (the installer fetches it if missing), internet for prices. Cougars, vJoy, HOTAS and a headset are all optional.
  • Send it to a friend: give them the zip (or the GitHub link). They run install.bat. That's it; it updates itself afterwards.
  • Updates: every launch checks GitHub. Settings → Check / Update & restart does it by hand. Your settings, macros, voice clips, cache and keybind backups are never overwritten.
  • Where things live: %LOCALAPPDATA%\ARIA — config.json (settings), macros.json, cache/, backups/ (your actionmaps before every change), aria.log.
  • Running without installing: start.bat in the unzipped folder.
