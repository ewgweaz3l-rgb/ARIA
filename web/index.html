Field notes

The bits that don't come from an API. Verify against the current patch — these drift.
  • Scan first. Ping (V by default), then target the rock: mass, resistance, instability, and the composition. High resistance wants more laser power or a modifier; high instability wants a steadier hand and gadgets.
  • Green window. Keep the charge bar in the green band; overcharge cracks the rock and you lose the good stuff. Add laser power slowly, back off early.
  • Fracture, then extract. After the split, switch to extraction and vacuum the fragments; the bags fill in cSCU (100 cSCU = 1 SCU).
  • Inert is dead weight. Dump inert material at the refinery (or jettison) — it takes bag space and pays nothing.
  • Refine or sell raw? Refined pays more per SCU but costs time and a fee, and the yield depends on method: fast/cheap methods lose material, slow/expensive ones keep it. The calculator on the left does the arithmetic with whatever yield you set.
  • Refinery picks. Each refinery is better at some ores than others (the yield column). A few % on Quantainium is real money; on cheap ore it's not worth the detour.
  • Quantainium is unstable. Sell or refine it fast and fly gently — the timer starts when it hits the bag.
  • Hand mining (Pyro, caves, some surface deposits) feeds the same commodity list in small SCU; the board's Hand / FPS filter shows those.
